# OS-Question

**Exploring Operating Systems, AI, and the structure beneath computation.**

---

## What is OS-Question?

OS-Question is a personal research space focused on:

- Operating system abstractions
- AI systems and agent runtimes
- Infrastructure, scheduling, and resource models
- The philosophical and structural questions beneath computation

This site serves as a long-term notebook, not a blog platform.

---

## Sections

- **[Questions](./questions/)**  
  Core questions and structured investigations. Each file contains problem statements, open-ended inquiries, or observed phenomena designed to provoke thought and further research.

- **[Notes](./notes/)**  
  Informal essays, experimental observations, and shorter reflections. This is where iterative thinking and early-stage ideas live.

- **[Explorations](./explorations/)**  
  Systematic documents, whitepapers, and research exports. Includes more formalized studies and reference material intended for reuse, citation, or deeper analysis.

- **[About](./about/)**  
  Background on the repository, purpose, and author intent.

---

## Philosophy

This repository is **not a blog or marketing channel**. It is a **research-oriented, problem-first workspace**.  
- Questions first, answers may follow.  
- Observations are structured, not sensational.  
- Ideas are public but purposefully restrained, to invite cognitive participation rather than casual consumption.

---

## How to Use

1. Browse **[Questions](./questions/)** for key open problems.  
2. Read **[Notes](./notes/)** for informal context and iterative thinking.  
3. Check **[Explorations](./explorations/)** for more systematic research.  

The repository is intended for long-term exploration of **what is AI role, intent sovereignty, and the future of systems**.
