# What Does It Mean When AI Is No Longer a Tool?

- Modern AI no longer feels like a tool that simply executes instructions.
- Interaction with AI increasingly resembles negotiation rather than command.
- Users adapt their language, context, and expectations to how the system interprets intent.
- This suggests a shift from execution-based software to interpretation-based systems.
- Interpretation introduces discretion, which is never neutral.
- When systems interpret intent, questions of control and power inevitably arise.
- Traditional software models did not need to address who "owns" interpretation.
- AI systems force this question to the surface.
- If AI is neither a tool nor a traditional operating system, what kind of system is it becoming?
- Why does this shift matter more than performance benchmarks or model size?
